Enter your code

Your teacher gave you a code. It is how the cards you write reach her without your name ever being in this page. If you do not have yours yet, ask her for it.

This browser will not let the page remember your code, so you will be asked for it again next time.

Your teacher sees your cards as you write them

Once you have entered your code, each card you write is sent a few seconds after you finish it, so you do not have to remember to save. Save my work at the top of the page does the same thing straight away, and is there for when you want to be sure.

What is sent is the cards you have written and the code you entered, and nothing else: not your name, not your email, not anything else on this page or in this browser. If you have not entered a code, nothing is sent at all.

Take your cards into Anki

Anki is free on computers and Android, and its scheduling is better than the review here. You write your cards in this page either way; Anki just becomes where you review them. There is no account to make and nothing to pay unless you want the iPhone app.

How do I import this?

Copy the block below. Paste it into a plain text file and save it as cards.txt, using Notepad on Windows or TextEdit in plain text mode on a Mac. Then in Anki choose File, then Import, and pick that file. Your cards arrive in a deck called AP Psychology, tagged by topic, so you can study one topic at a time.

Other ways to keep your cards

Hand this in

Copy everything in the box and paste it into your Question Notebook, under a heading called Card Bank. That is one way your teacher sees the cards you wrote. Saving does the same job in one press.

Bring a deck back

Cleared your browser, or moved to another device? Paste a block you exported earlier and your definitions and review progress come back.
