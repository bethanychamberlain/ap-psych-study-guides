    Whole units, for revision

    The week pages are what to do as you go. These are the same cards gathered by unit, which is how AP Classroom and the exam are organized, so this is where you check a unit is complete before a Progress Check. A card you write on any page shows up on all of them.

    The course's wording is deliberately hidden until you have written your own. Writing the card is where the learning happens. Reviewing someone else's card is not the same thing, and it does not stick.

    What each page does

    • Shows you this week's terms and asks you to write each definition in your own words
    • Brings back a term you met in an earlier week when we return to it, with the card you already wrote, so you sharpen it rather than start again
    • Lets you review only the cards you wrote yourself
    • Exports your deck as text, to paste into your Question Notebook
    • Exports the same deck in Anki's import format, if you use Anki

    Anki is still the better reviewer

    These pages exist because AnkiMobile costs money and some people will not install software. If you will install Anki, install Anki: its scheduling, syncing and offline app all beat this. Write your cards here, export them, and review them there.

    What leaves your browser, and what does not

    Each bank asks for the code your teacher gave you when you open it. Once it has your code, a card you finish is sent a few seconds later, so nobody loses work by forgetting to save. Save my work does the same thing straight away. What is sent is the cards you have written and your code, and nothing else. There are still no accounts and no sign-in: your name is not in the page, is not sent, and sits only in your teacher's own list beside your code.

    Until you enter a code, nothing is sent anywhere at all. Your cards are kept on your own device as well as sent, so closing the page never loses them.

    You need your code to open a bank, so ask for it if you do not have it yet. Copying the text into your Question Notebook does the same job as saving.
